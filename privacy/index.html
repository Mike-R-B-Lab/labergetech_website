Privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Who is responsible

Michael Laberge, founder of LabergeTech, is responsible for protecting personal information. For any question or request: michael@labergetech.com.

What we collect

  • Through the invoicing questionnaire: your first name, your company name, your email, your phone number if you give it, and your answers to the questions.
  • Through the contact form: your name, your email and your message.

Why

To produce your result, email it to you, answer your request and follow up with you about it. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for anything else without your consent.

Who can see it

Only Michael Laberge. The information is stored in Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Gmail). Contact form messages go through Web3Forms. These services may store data outside Quebec and Canada.

How long we keep it

No more than 24 months after our last exchange, then it's deleted.

Cookies and tracking

The site uses no cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records the page viewed, the referring site, and the browser, device type and country, without cookies and without identifying you. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which sends your IP address to Google.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to michael@labergetech.com. We reply within 30 days. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
